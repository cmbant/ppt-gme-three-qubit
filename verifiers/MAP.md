# Verification tags and scripts

Each tag of the paper's verification section, with the scripts (in `code/`) and certificate data that back it. A script's
captured output is `verification/<script>.txt`. The first rows are the groups of the paper's Appendix on proof dependencies;
the tagged rows are the finer checks, and the claim-class column of the README names the tags each result uses.
The `explore_*` scripts give numerical evidence or search for certificate data and back no proof; they rewrite the data
files they produce, so run them on a copy of `code/`.

## What each tag checks

- **Mixed $W$ class**: Theorem 12 is analytic. The finite cyclic/parity decompositions, zero-hyperdeterminant identities, surface coefficient budgets, phase-coexistence line, and full-rank state $\tilde\rho$ are independently checked in exact arithmetic. The check includes both surface branches, the phase-coexistence line, $\tilde\rho$, nontrivial local filters, and a GHZ negative control; no numerical convex-roof minimisation is used.
- **Extremality and moduli**: The extremality of Corollary 5 rests on an exact $24$-of-$25$ rank calculation. For every state of the $(5,5,5,5)$ surface, the extremality of Corollary 8 is proved from the $2\times4$ low-rank theorem, and the same symmetry-block minors, explicit polynomials in $Q$, give an independent check of it and the $27$ independent constraint differentials of Proposition 9, checked symbolically and by an exact rank computation at $Q=2$; the local-filter orbit has dimension $19$ because the stabiliser of the range is finite (Corollary 4(b)). The proofs are in Appendices A and B. These results are computer-assisted.
- **One support, both phases**: Theorem 14 and Proposition 15 rest on exact rational expansions of finite cut-product mixtures, principal minors of the partial-transpose parity blocks, witness values and a Hilbert--Schmidt bound (Appendix C).
- **Noise bounds**: The level-two certificates use rational matrices $Y$, $Z_1$, $Z_2$ given in symmetry-adapted coordinates (cyclic symmetry, global parity and the antiunitary $K\circ S_{BC}$): $Y$ has $10$ real parameters and each $Z_i$ is a pair of real symmetric $8\times8$ blocks ($72$ parameters). The required positivity conditions and polynomial identities are verified in exact arithmetic. The certificate data consist of the witnesses for the family, the state $\tilde\rho$ with its witness, and the witness $Y_*$ for the surface state. Every white-noise bound, radius and table entry of Section 6 (proofs in Appendix D) is checked from these data, not inferred from solver output; this includes the spectral enclosure $-I/4\prec Y_*\prec31I/10$, the rational value of $\operatorname{tr}(Y_*\sigma)$, the partial-transpose eigenvalue bounds and the use of $\sqrt2<99/70$ in the PPT radii. Negative controls lie just beyond each stated bound.
- **Sharp white-noise thresholds**: Proposition 21 uses explicit biseparable decompositions, stored as three orbit representatives per state and expanded under parity and $K\circ S_{BC}$. The exact checks cover positive definiteness of every $\Lambda_r$, cyclic invariance of the residual and the Gurvits--Barnum inequality, with a negative control at the certified GME noise level.
- **CF-1–CF-6**: The family of Section 2: the orthogonal five-component family, cyclic and parity symmetry, all partial-transpose minors and the exact PPT interval (CF-1); the complete constant-rank cut-product curve, including $z=0$ and $z=\infty$, with cyclic transport to the other cuts (CF-2); the supported witnesses $H_s$ and the factorised $H_5=H_1$ that detects the whole PPT interval (CF-3); the unique GHZ and product spectral rays and the nonreal phase projectors, an independent check of Corollary 4(a) inside the PPT interval (CF-4); the conditioning determinant $\ge1/81$, the penalty $202905$, the radius $1/20\,000\,000$ and the partial-transpose margin $1/2000$ of the hand-checkable remark (CF-5); and the exact algebraic rank $24$ of the extremality system of Corollary 5 (CF-6).
- **IN-1–IN-7**: An independent recomputation of Theorem 1, which does not reuse CF, at each of the three cuts separately and without cyclic transport: $KB=0$, $\operatorname{rank} K=3$, $B^\dagger B=\operatorname{diag}(4,1,3,3,3)$ (IN-1); no common zero of the $3\times3$ minors, rank three at infinity, and the kernel vector expanded in $B$ (IN-2); the witness identity (6) and the value at infinity (IN-3); the identity of Proposition 2(b) for every $s>0$, recomputed from the printed $q_s(z)$ (IN-4); $\operatorname{tr}(H_5X)=(29-9t)/(7(t+3))$ and $t_->29/9$ (IN-5); the exact PPT interval from the nonnegativity sets of the characteristic-polynomial coefficients, with the endpoint determinants (IN-6); and a negative control, $H_5$ with its $w$-weight lowered from $11$ to $10$, which fails (IN-7).
- **FP-1–FP-5**: The rank-$(5,5,5,5)$ support surface and the explicit state: the symbolic identities of Theorem 7(a) in $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$ (FP-1); exact positivity, rank five, cyclic and parity invariance and partial-transpose ranks of $\sigma$ (FP-2); the level-two certificate and the value $\operatorname{tr}(Y_*\sigma)$ with the white-noise bound, exactly $g>407/100000$ (FP-3); the exact rank $24$ of $25$ of the extremality system (FP-4); and two negative controls, one leaving the surface and one a fully-PPT surface point where $Y_*$ does not detect (FP-5).
- **SW-1–SW-8**: The analytic supported witnesses on the surface: completeness of the cut-product rays (SW-1); identity (5), its gauge reduction to $s=1$ and cyclic invariance (SW-2); the witness $H_{1/2}$ and the value $-493577/32646438$ of $\operatorname{tr}(HX_\sigma)$ for Corollary 8 (SW-3); the local-filter orbit (SW-4); the positivity and partial-transpose window via the Gram factor and leading minors (SW-5); the genuine-entanglement region $3/4\le\tau<1$ via the factorised $\operatorname{tr}(H_5X_\tau)$, the polynomial $S_6$ and its shifted expansion with the sign bound (SW-6); the form $H_0$ of Lemma 6, its axis-interpolation identity, the factorised $\operatorname{tr}(H_0X_\tau)$ that closes the window, the four-term single-cut product decomposition of the lower endpoint $\rho_{1/\sqrt3}$, and the uniqueness of $H_0$ from the six endpoint double roots (SW-7); and the rank-two fully separable upper endpoint at every filter parameter, including the exact limit, product decomposition and all partial transposes (SW-8).
- **SB-1–SB-4**: Corollary 8 and Proposition 9 on the whole surface: the polynomial kernel $K_A(Q)$ of $\rho_\tau^{T_A}$ (SB-1); the six extremality block minors, no root in the window (SB-2); the four constraint-differential block minors (SB-3); and an exact check at $Q=2$ without symmetry reduction, ranks $24$, $27$ and $19$ (SB-4).
- **EO-1–EO-5**: Remark 11: the product vectors and their partial conjugates, the overlap with $k_1$, the values of the overlap with $k_3$ on the two branches, the signs of the factors on the window, and the zeros at the two endpoints.
- **SC-1–SC-5**: Corollary 4(b): the determinant $-3(z^2+s)^2$ and the three fully product rays of $R_s$ (SC-1, SC-2); the cyclic-shift eigenspaces of $R_s$ and $\overline{R_s}$ (SC-3); the $S_3$ stabiliser and the gauge $R_s=\operatorname{diag}(1,\sqrt s)^{\otimes3}R_1$ (SC-4, SC-5).
- **MO-1–MO-4**: Proposition 9: exact complex ranks over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ of the complexified rank-five tangent map ($55$), the three partial-transpose kernels (dimension $3$), the $27$ Schur-complement differentials ($27$), and the local-filter orbit map ($19$, inside the tangent).
- **PG-2, PG-4**: Section 5: the exact $13$-term cyclic and parity biseparable decomposition and rank-five coefficient matrix of $\sigma_{\rm BS}$, its strict PPT by principal minors, the same-support witness values (PG-2); the exposed mixture $\sigma_\partial$ with $\operatorname{tr}(H_5X_\partial)=0$, its shifted Sylvester minors and the exact phase line (PG-4).
- **DS-1–DS-7**: The level-two certificates of Section 6: the extension identity (all $144$ coefficients), cyclic symmetry, positivity of $Z_1$, $Z_2$, $P$ and a negative control, for each design value of $t$ and for the robust state $\tilde\rho$ (DS-1); every tolerance of the table, as exact rational or algebraic numbers (DS-2); negativity on the rational bracket and the uniform interval bounds (DS-3); full rank, cyclic and parity symmetry, the partial-transpose lower bounds and the tolerances of $\tilde\rho$ (DS-4); as numerical evidence only, the optimal tolerances of the relaxation (DS-5); the bounds of Corollary 19 from the witness $Y_*$ (DS-6); and the explicit biseparable decompositions bounding the white-noise thresholds from above (DS-7).

## Provenance of the certificate data

The generator `explore_ppt_gme_sym_dps2.py` solves the level-two dual directly in the symmetry-adapted coordinates (the optimal value is unchanged by the symmetry reduction). The witness $Y_*$ is optimal for $\rho_*$ under $-I/4\preceq Y_*\preceq 31I/10$ and $\operatorname{tr}(Y_*\sigma)\le-0.004076$. The state $\tilde\rho$ comes from the cyclic, parity and $K\circ S_{BC}$ symmetric seesaw, started from a five-term ansatz on $u_s,w+cv,e,\bar e,o$ read off an exploratory random-start run; it converges to white-noise tolerance $2.36\times10^{-2}$, and mixing white noise of weight $2.5\times10^{-4}$ and rounding gives $\tilde\rho$. These are search results, not certificates of a local or global optimum.
